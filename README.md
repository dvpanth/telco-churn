# Telco Customer Churn
This telecommunications company provides services such as internet and cell service to their customers, an essential with the era of modern-day technology. However, some customers end up cancelling their subscription, raising some challenges, the most prominent of them being

- Lost revenue: clients provide a monthly/annual subscription fee, losing clients means a lost stream of revenue.
- Customer acquisition costs: replacing the lost customers with new ones may require expensive marketing expenditure
- Customer retention: providing extra, targeted support to customers likely to churn could prevent them from leaving.

The scope of this project is to develop a machine learning classification model capable of identifying customers with a high risk of churning, that way the company can employ retention efforts towards customers most likely to leave. Model performance will be evaluated based on metrics accounting for missed churners and unnecessary retention efforts, with the ultimate goal of supporting a more cost-effective retention strategy.
